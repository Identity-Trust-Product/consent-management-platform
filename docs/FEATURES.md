# Key Features

Open Bharart Digital Consent is a comprehensive Consent Management Platform (CMP) designed to handle complex privacy workflows.

## 1. Consent Management
-   **Lifecycle Management**: Track consent from grant to revocation.
-   **Granular Control**: Manage consents at the Business Process, Purpose, and Attribute levels.
-   **Versioning**: Track changes to consent terms and trigger re-consent workflows when necessary.

## 2. Privacy Notices
-   **Dynamic Generation**: Create privacy notices tailored to specific business processes.
-   **Multi-Language Support**: Automatically translate notices into multiple languages using Google Translate integration.
-   **Delivery**: Send notices via Email or SMS (extensible).

## 3. Grievance Redressal
-   **Ticketing System**: Data Principals can raise grievances regarding their data.
-   **SLA Tracking**: Monitor grievance resolution times against Service Level Agreements.
-   **File Attachments**: Securely upload evidence or documents related to grievances.
-   **Workflow**: Status tracking (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed).

## 4. Data Principal Rights Management (DPRM)
-   **Self-Service Portal**: A dedicated portal for Data Principals to view and manage their consents.
-   **Revocation**: Easy one-click revocation of previously granted consents.
-   **Access Logs**: View a history of all consent-related activities.

## 5. Integration & Extensibility
-   **Webhooks**: Real-time event notifications for external systems (e.g., when consent is revoked).
-   **API Access**: Secure v1 API for programmatic access to consent data.
-   **Data Processors**: Registry of third-party data processors to ensure full supply chain compliance.

## 6. Security & Compliance
-   **RBAC**: Granular Role-Based Access Control for Admins, Data Fiduciaries, and Users.
-   **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive logs of all critical system actions.
-   **API Key Management**: Secure authentication for machine-to-machine integrations.
